A note from us　／　ご挨拶

We found our spot in Yoyogi Park — and you found us.

What started as a small gathering to mark the launch of Studio Kaku became something we'll carry with us for a long time. The food, the flowers, the afternoon light coming through the trees, the dog introductions. So many of you came, and we are genuinely, deeply grateful.

The launch went beyond anything we had hoped for. To see our friends and community show up so warmly for what we are building here means everything.

4月26日、代々木公園で私たちの場所を見つけました。そして皆さんも、私たちを見つけてくれました。Studio Kakuのローンチを祝うために集まったこの日は、きっと長く記憶に残るものになるでしょう。心からありがとうございました。
